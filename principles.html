OUR PRINCIPLES

Understand the ideas.
Then participate.

Rooks of Honor provides educational material around constitutional government, individual liberty, privacy, civic participation, and responsible engagement with public policy.

01

Constitutional Government

Learn what the Constitution says, how governmental powers are structured, and where to find primary sources.

02

Privacy & Civil Liberties

Explore the legal and historical foundations of privacy, speech, due process, and limits on government power.

03

Informed Participation

Voting is one part of civic life. Public meetings, contacting officials, volunteering, and learning local government matter too.

04

Foreign Policy Literacy

Learn how Congress, the executive branch, international law, and U.S. foreign assistance intersect.
