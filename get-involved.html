GET INVOLVED

Start where
you are.

Civic participation can be practical and local. Learn how your community works and take part in the public processes that affect your neighbors.

Attend public meetings

City councils, county commissions, school boards, and other public bodies often publish meeting schedules and agendas.

Learn to vote

Use official state and local election resources for registration, voting methods, deadlines, and polling information.

Volunteer

Consider nonpartisan civic opportunities such as poll-worker programs and community organizations.

Contact officials

Learn who represents you and how to communicate clearly and respectfully about issues that matter to you.

Official election information

The U.S. Election Assistance Commission provides state-specific voter resources and information about becoming a poll worker.
